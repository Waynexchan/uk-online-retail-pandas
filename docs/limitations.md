# Limitations

This analysis should be interpreted with the following limitations in mind:

- The dataset is historical and may not represent current customer or product behaviour.
- The dataset does not include marketing cost data, so marketing return on investment cannot be calculated.
- The dataset does not include product cost data, so gross margin or profit cannot be calculated.
- The dataset does not include customer demographic data.
- Missing customer IDs limit customer-level analysis.
- Cancelled invoices and returns require careful handling and should not be mixed into valid sales without clear logic.
- Product descriptions may need additional standardisation.
- Some stock codes may represent postage, discounts, manual adjustments, or non-product transactions.
- Incomplete months can distort monthly comparisons.
- Findings should be validated with additional business context before being used for business decisions.
