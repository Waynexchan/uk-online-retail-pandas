## Executive Summary

This business operates primarily as a B2B wholesale retailer, with the top 20% of customers contributing approximately 77% of total revenue. Revenue concentration indicates strong dependence on key wholesale accounts.

Return analysis shows that overall return rates remain moderate and stable for most customers (median 1.45%, 75% below 3%). Although certain customers exhibit extremely high return rates, these are driven by bulk order reversals and commercial adjustments rather than widespread product quality issues.

Given the high revenue concentration among wholesale customers, return behavior and purchasing policies of key accounts significantly impact profitability. 

It is recommended to:
- Optimize wholesale return policies (e.g., clearer return windows and seasonal restrictions),
- Strengthen key account management,
- Introduce loyalty or incentive programs to stabilize order volumes while controlling return risk.

This strategic focus would better protect margins while supporting long-term wholesale relationships.

## Key Findings

- The business operates primarily as a B2B wholesale model, with the top 20% of customers contributing ~77% of total revenue.
- Sales exhibit strong seasonality, peaking between August and November, with November as the highest revenue month.
- Return behavior is not systemic; median customer return rate is 1.45%, with most returns driven by bulk wholesale order reversals.
- Product-level analysis shows no evidence of widespread quality issues.

---

## Business Implications

- Revenue and return risk are highly concentrated among a small number of wholesale customers.
- Wholesale return behavior has a disproportionate impact on profitability, especially around seasonal inventory cycles.
- Operational efficiency during peak periods is critical to capturing full revenue potential.

---

## Recommendations

1. **Optimize Wholesale Return Policies**  
   - Introduce clearer return windows aligned with seasonal inventory cycles.  
   - Limit late-season or post-season bulk returns to reduce obsolete stock risk.

2. **Strengthen Key Account Management**  
   - Prioritize relationship management for top 20% revenue customers.  
   - Monitor order reversal patterns and proactively engage high-risk accounts.

3. **Introduce Wholesale Loyalty & Incentive Programs**  
   - Offer volume-based incentives to stabilize demand.  
   - Encourage early-season purchasing with favorable pricing to improve inventory planning.

---

## Next Steps

- Conduct deeper customer segmentation within the top 20% to identify distinct wholesale behavior patterns.
- Analyze margin impact of returns by product category.
- Integrate inventory availability and lead-time data to refine seasonal planning.
