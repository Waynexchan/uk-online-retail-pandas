## Sales Trend

### Recommendation1 
Given the strong seasonal peak in November, the company should reinforce marketing efforts during Black Friday and pre-Christmas campaigns to maximize revenue. Inventory planning should begin as early as October to ensure sufficient stock availability before peak demand. Additionally, promotional campaigns in January and February could help mitigate the post-holiday sales slump and clear excess winter inventory, preparing the business for the next seasonal cycle.

### Peak Season Recommendation
Since the November peak is primarily volume-driven, operational readiness becomes critical. Inventory availability should be prioritized to prevent stock-outs during high demand periods. Additionally, ensuring checkout system efficiency is essential to maintain conversion rates and avoid revenue loss due to system overload.
