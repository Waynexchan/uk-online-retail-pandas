# Ask

## Business Task
Analyse UK online retail transaction data to understand sales performance and customer behaviour, and provide insights to support business decision-making.

## Stakeholders
- Sales Manager
- Operations Team
- Management Team

## Key Questions
1. How has sales performance changed over time?
2. Which products and customers contribute most to revenue?
3. Are there any notable patterns in cancellations or returns?

## Success Criteria
- Clear identification of sales trends
- Actionable insights for improving revenue or operations
- Findings communicated in a clear, non-technical way
